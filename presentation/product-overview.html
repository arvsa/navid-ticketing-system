Product overview

One place for every request, task, report and event across the school

A web app that lets branch leaders hand out work, follow it to completion and see what's happening at every site, live. Staff ask questions, submit work and stay informed without chasing emails.

The problem

Today, work across branches travels through email threads, chat messages and spreadsheets. That causes the same problems again and again.

Requests get lost

Questions and tasks are buried in inboxes. Nobody can tell what is still open or who owns it.

No overview

School leaders can't see how each branch is doing without asking for updates.

Chasing for reports

Collecting a report from every teacher means reminders, spreadsheets and manual tracking.

Unclear sign-off

It isn't recorded who approved what, or when.

Who it's for

The school is organized into branches, and each branch into groups of staff. People can work at more than one branch.

Super admin

School leadership. Sets up branches and their admins, sends work to every branch at once, signs off on school-wide tasks and sees everything.

Branch admin

Runs a branch. Manages its staff and groups, decides what each group may do, hands out work and approves it.

Staff

Teachers and support staff. Ask questions, receive and complete tasks, write reports, create events and reply to invitations.

Admins can give a group extra powers, such as approving work or exporting data, without making its members full admins. See the Permissions document for details.

What it does

Tickets for questions and requests

Anyone can ask a question and address it to a person or group. The answer comes in comments, and the person who asked closes it when satisfied.

Actions: tasks with sign-off

Send a task to a person or a group. Choose whether one group member picks it up, or everyone gets their own copy. Work is submitted, then approved or sent back with a note.

Reports, collected and approved

Request a written report from a group. Each person drafts, submits and gets approval. You can see who has submitted and who hasn't, without chasing anyone.

Events with RSVPs

Calendar entries like Outlook's: invite people or groups, collect going / maybe / declined replies, and send automatic reminders. Invitees are told when the time changes or the event is cancelled.

Branches and groups

Each branch runs itself. Admins create groups (departments, year teams, committees), send work to whole groups at once, and give groups the permissions they need.

Private items

Sensitive matters can be marked private, so only the people involved and admins can see them. Comments can also be marked staff-only.

Deadlines and overdue alerts

Tasks and reports have due dates. People are warned the day before, and admins see anything overdue straight away.

Comments, mentions and files

Discuss work in place, @mention colleagues to bring them in, and attach documents or photos.

In-app notifications

A personal inbox on the dashboard for new assignments, mentions, approvals, deadlines and event reminders. It arrives instantly, without email.

Excel export

Admins download their branch's tickets, actions, reports and events for any date range. Super admins can export any branch, or the whole school.

Full history

Every change, approval, permission change and export is recorded permanently, so there's always an answer to "who did this, and when?".

The live dashboard

Everyone lands on a dashboard that updates by itself the moment something changes, with no refreshing. Admins see their whole branch, super admins see every branch, and staff see their own work. The example below is illustrative.

A week in the app

Monday

The super admin sends a fire-drill Action to every branch, due Friday. Each branch admin is notified straight away.

Monday

The North admin assigns the drill to the site manager, and sends a term Report request to the Science group, with one copy per teacher.

Tuesday

A teacher opens a Ticket asking where to order lab supplies, addressed to the Lab technicians group. A technician replies in the comments, and the teacher closes it.

Wednesday

The admin creates a Parent Evening event and invites all staff. RSVPs come in, and a reminder goes out the day before.

Thursday

Teachers submit their reports. The admin approves most of them and sends one back with a note. The dashboard shows two reports still outstanding, and those teachers get a due-tomorrow reminder.

Friday

Every branch has submitted its drill, and a super admin approves them all. At the end of term, the admin exports the branch's reports to Excel.

What's in the first version

Included

  • Branches, members (including people in several branches) and groups
  • Configurable permissions per group, with safe defaults
  • Tickets, actions, reports and events
  • Approvals, deadlines and overdue alerts
  • RSVPs and event reminders
  • Comments, @mentions and file attachments
  • Private items and staff-only comments
  • Live dashboard and in-app notifications
  • Excel export
  • Complete activity history

Not yet (possible later)

  • Email or text-message notifications
  • Self-service password reset (admins reset passwords for now)
  • Recurring tasks and events, such as a weekly report
  • Reporting by academic term (exports use date ranges)
  • Advanced search
  • Calendar sync with Outlook or Google Calendar
  • Mobile app (the web app works on phones)
