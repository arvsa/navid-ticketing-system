What the system keeps track of

A plain-language tour of the information the app stores, how the pieces connect, and the rules the database enforces so the data stays trustworthy.

The big picture

The school is organized into branches, and people work in branches and their groups. Work is created as items, which are sent to people or groups. Everything said or attached to an item stays with it, and every change is recorded.

Four kinds of items

Every piece of work in the app is an item. The four kinds share the same basics (title, description, priority, who it is sent to) but behave differently.

Ticket

A question or request, such as "Where do I order lab supplies?". It is answered in comments, and whoever asked closes it.

Action

A task sent to people or groups, such as "Update the fire-exit signs". Each person's work is submitted, then approved.

Report

Like an action, but the person must attach a written report before submitting. Anyone can also file a report of their own.

Event

A calendar entry with invitees, such as "Parent evening, Thursday 6pm". Invitees reply going, maybe or declined, and get reminders.

The building blocks

Each card is one kind of record the database keeps. The grey label is its technical name, which is useful when talking to developers.

People and organisation

People app_user

Everyone who can sign in: name, email and a securely scrambled password. People are deactivated, never deleted, so their history stays intact.

Super admins are marked here. Their access covers every branch.

Branches branch

Each school site, with a short code, a name and its time zone, so deadlines and event times are right for that site.

Example: NTH, North Campus, Europe/London

Branch membership branch_membership

Links a person to a branch. A person can belong to several branches. This is also where someone is made the admin of a branch.

Omar: admin at North, regular member at South

Groups user_group

Teams inside one branch, created by the branch admin. Items can be sent to a whole group, and a group can be given extra permissions.

Every branch has an automatic All members group.

Group members group_member

Who is in each group. Joining a group immediately gives that person the group's permissions, and leaving takes them away.

Group permissions group_permission

What members of a group may do, such as "approve work" or "export to Excel". The full list is in the companion permissions document.

Work

Items issue

The ticket, action, report or event itself: title, description, priority, whether it is private, and whether it is open, closed or cancelled. Each gets a friendly number such as ISS-1042.

Item kinds issue_type

The four kinds, and each one's default deadline. For example, actions are due 3 days after sending unless set otherwise.

Recipients issue_target

Who an item is sent to: a person, a group, or (for super admins only) an entire branch. One item can have many recipients.

Removing a recipient cancels their unfinished work but keeps the record. They can be added again later.

Assignments work_item

The actual piece of work for actions and reports: who is doing it, its status and its deadline. It also records who approved or rejected it, and why.

When an item is sent to a group, it can be shared (one person picks it up) or one each (everyone in the group gets their own).

If someone leaves the branch, their unfinished work becomes unassigned and the branch admins are told, so it can be handed to someone else.

Watchers issue_watcher

People who asked to be kept informed about an item without working on it.

Read markers issue_read

When each person last opened an item. This powers the "unread" badges on the dashboard.

Conversation and content

Comments issue_comment

The discussion on an item, or on one person's assignment. A comment can be marked staff-only so ordinary members don't see it.

Mentions comment_mention

People @mentioned in a comment. Each one gets a notification.

Written reports issue_report

The written report, saved as a draft until submitted. Required before a Report assignment can be handed in.

Files issue_attachment

Uploaded documents and photos, attached to the item, a comment or a report. Files can only be downloaded by people allowed to see the item.

Calendar

Event details issue_event

Start and end time, all-day or not, and location.

RSVPs event_rsvp

One per invitee: waiting, going, maybe or declined, with an optional note.

Reminders event_reminder

When to remind invitees, for example 1 day before and 1 hour before.

Keeping people informed, and keeping records

Notifications notification

The inbox on each person's dashboard: newly assigned work, mentions, approvals, deadlines approaching, event reminders and more. Read or unread.

Activity history audit_log

A permanent, unchangeable record of every important change: status updates, approvals, permission changes, new admins, exports. Nobody can edit or delete it.

Sign-in sessions user_session

Who is currently signed in, and on which device. Sessions expire, and an admin can sign someone out everywhere.

The life of an assignment

Actions and reports follow this path for each person doing the work.

Open→ In progress⇄ Blocked→ Waiting for approval→ Approved

If the approver rejects the work, it goes back to In progress with a note explaining why. Any unfinished assignment can be Cancelled. When every assignment on an item is approved or cancelled, the item closes by itself.

KindHow it finishes
TicketThe person who asked closes it once answered. An admin can close it too.
ActionEach assignment is submitted and approved. The item closes when all are done.
ReportSame as an action, but a written report must be attached before submitting. Reports someone files for themselves can be closed by that person.
EventThe organizer closes it after it happens, or cancels it. Cancelling tells every invitee.

Rules the database guarantees

These are enforced by the database itself, so no bug in the app can break them. Each one has been tested.

  • Every branch always has at least one admin. A branch can't be created without one, and the last admin can't be removed until a replacement is appointed.
  • There is always at least one active super admin.
  • Everyone who joins a branch is automatically put in its All members group, and that group can't be deleted.
  • Leaving a branch removes the person from all of that branch's groups and ends their access to its items, including items they created or worked on. Their membership in other branches is untouched.
  • Work can only be assigned to people who belong to that branch.
  • An item belonging to one branch can only be sent to people and groups in that branch. Only super admins can send items to whole branches or across branches.
  • Work can't be approved before it has been submitted.
  • A permission can only be given in a form that makes sense. For example, "export" can't be limited to one kind of item.
  • The activity history can never be edited or deleted.
  • Two people editing the same item at once can't silently overwrite each other. The second one is told to refresh.
  • Nothing important is ever deleted: people are deactivated, members "leave", recipients are "removed", and items are closed or cancelled. History stays complete.

Glossary

BranchA school site. Most permissions apply to one branch only.
Super adminSchool-wide administrator with access to every branch.
Branch adminRuns one branch. Appointed only by a super admin.
GroupA team inside a branch, used to send work to many people at once and to give extra permissions.
ItemA ticket, action, report or event.
RecipientA person, group or branch an item is sent to.
AssignmentOne person's (or one group's) piece of work on an action or report.
Private itemVisible only to its creator, its recipients and admins.
Staff-only commentA comment hidden from ordinary members.
Deadline (SLA)When an assignment is due. The dashboard flags anything overdue.
