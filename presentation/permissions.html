Who can do what

How access works across branches, groups and roles. It covers what everyone can do by default, what admins can hand out, and the few things that stay locked to admins and super admins.

Three levels of access

Access always belongs to a branch. Someone who works in two branches can have different powers in each. Being an admin at North gives nothing extra at South.

Whole school

Super admin

Can do everything, in every branch.
  • Creates branches and appoints or removes branch admins
  • Sends work to whole branches, or to several at once
  • The only one who approves work they (or another super admin) sent out
  • Exports data for any or all branches
One branch

Branch admin

Runs their branch. Appointed by a super admin.
  • Manages members, groups and each group's permissions
  • Can see, edit, close and approve all work in the branch
  • On super admin items: can comment and organize their branch's part, but not edit or approve
  • Exports their branch's data
One branch

Members

Everyone else. Their powers come from their groups.
  • By default: see the branch's non-private items, ask questions, file reports, create events, and work on what they're given
  • Close what they created
  • Can be given more, up to nearly admin level, by joining a group the admin has set up

How groups give permissions

Groups are teams inside a branch, for example "Science teachers", "Year 10 tutors" or "Coordinators". Only the branch admin creates them.

  1. The admin creates a group and chooses what its members may do, for example "approve work" or "export to Excel".
  2. The admin adds people to the group. They get those permissions immediately, in that branch only.
  3. Someone in several groups gets everything from all of them combined.
  4. When someone is removed from the group, the permissions are gone on their very next click. No sign-out needed.
  5. Anyone can see the list of their own permissions and which group each one comes from. Admins can see this for every member.
The "All members" group. Every branch has one automatically, and everyone in the branch is in it. It holds the everyday permissions every member gets. The admin can adjust what it allows, but can't delete it.

Reach: how far a permission goes

Most permissions come with a reach, so an admin can give "close their own items" without giving "close anyone's items".

Their ownItems the person created themselves.
Sent to themItems sent to them directly, to a group they're in, or work assigned to them.
Whole branchEvery item in the branch.

Permissions can also be limited to one kind of item. For example, a group could approve Reports but not Actions.

Locked actions

An admin can give a group every permission in the table, making its members almost admins within that branch. These actions can never be handed out.

🔒 Super admins only

  • Create, rename or close branches
  • Appoint or remove branch admins
  • Make someone a super admin
  • Send work to whole branches, or to several branches at once
  • Approve, edit or close work a super admin sent out
  • Export more than one branch at a time
  • Change anything about a super admin's account
  • Deactivate the account of an admin, or of someone in more than one branch

🔒 Branch admins only

  • Create, edit or delete groups
  • Add or remove people from groups
  • Decide what each group may do, including the "All members" defaults
  • Change anything about another admin's account
  • Bring back a member who left the branch
  • Cancel one person's assignment on its own

Safety rules

These always apply and can't be switched off.

  • Every branch always has an admin. A branch is created together with its first admin, and the last admin can't be removed until a super admin appoints another.
  • There is always at least one super admin.
  • Nobody approves their own work. Approval always comes from someone else.
  • Private means private. A private item is seen only by its creator, the people it was sent to, and admins. Other members can't tell it exists.
  • Staff-only comments stay hidden from people without permission everywhere: on screen, in notifications and in exports.
  • Changes take effect immediately. Removing someone from a group or a branch removes their access on their next click.
  • Leaving a branch ends all access to it, including items the person created or worked on there. Their unfinished work becomes unassigned so an admin can hand it on.
  • Everything is on record. Every change to permissions, groups, admins and exports is written to a permanent history, and branch admins are notified when a group's permissions change.
  • Owners are told. If an admin edits or closes someone else's item, the creator is notified.

Examples

Sara, a teacher at North, has a question about lab supplies.
Allowed.She creates a Ticket and sends it to the "Lab technicians" group. When she has her answer, she closes it herself.
The North admin needs every science teacher to hand in a term report.
Allowed.She sends a Report to the "Science" group as one each. Every teacher gets their own assignment, writes the report and submits it. The admin approves each one or sends it back.
The super admin schedules a fire drill at every branch.
Allowed.The Action goes to all branches. Each branch admin assigns their part to someone, and only a super admin can approve the completed drills.
Omar is admin at North and a teacher at South. Can he approve work at South?
No.Admin powers belong to North only. At South he has the same permissions as any other teacher there.
The North admin sets up "Coordinators" with power to approve work and manage members. Can a coordinator create a new group?
No.Coordinators can approve work and add or remove ordinary members. Creating groups, changing group permissions and appointing admins stay with the admin and super admin.
A counsellor's referral is marked private. Can other teachers see it?
No.Only the person who created it, the people it was sent to, and admins can see it. It doesn't appear in anyone else's lists, searches, dashboard or exports.
The only admin at South is leaving. Can they be removed?
Not yet.The system refuses until a super admin appoints a new admin for South. This keeps a branch from ending up with no admin.
Can a teacher close an Action an admin assigned to them?
No, they submit it instead.They mark their work as done and submit it for approval. The admin approves it, and the item closes once everyone's work is approved.
